A NOTE FROM US

More than a cafe.
A feeling.

Some places are made to be more than just a place to eat. CafeOteca is one such place, jahan good food, refreshing drinks aur good vibes ek saath milte hain.

We believe ki achha food aur beautiful moments ke liye hamesha zyada spend karna zaroori nahi hota. Sometimes, a cup of chai, your favourite snack and the right company are all you need.

Whether it’s a quick coffee break, long conversations with friends, a casual date or simply some me-time, we hope every visit gives you a moment worth taking home.
